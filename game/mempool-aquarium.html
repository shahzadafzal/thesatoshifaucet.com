🐋 How the Mempool Aquarium works

What you're looking at

Every fish is a real, unconfirmed Bitcoin transaction that is waiting in the mempool — the queue of payments miners haven't put into a block yet. Your browser asks mempool.space for the newest ones about every 4 seconds, so nothing here loads our server.

How to play

  • Tap or click a fish to catch it. Aim a little generously — fingers are fat.
  • Your score is the total value you've caught, shown in sats and BTC (1 BTC = 100,000,000 sats). One whale can outweigh hundreds of shrimp.
  • Streaks: catch fish in quick succession (about 2 seconds apart) and the catch sounds climb in pitch. It's just for fun — it doesn't change your score.
  • After a catch, tap View ↗ to open that exact transaction on mempool.space.
  • Tap the corner arrows button for full screen (on iPhone it expands the game to fill the screen instead).

Fish types

    Size & colour

    • Size follows the logarithm of the value, so a 1 BTC fish isn't a thousand times bigger than a 0.001 BTC one — it would never fit on screen.
    • Colour shows the fee rate: blue = cheap fee (patient sender), green / yellow = paying more, orange / red = paying a premium to jump the queue. Whales are always gold.

    Good to know

    • Whales are rare. mempool.space only hands over the 10 newest transactions per request, so you see a live sample, not every payment. A real 1 BTC+ transaction turns up only now and then — you'll hear a sonar ping and see a banner when one appears.
    • The value shown is the transaction's total outputs, which includes any change sent back to the sender — so a fish can look bigger than what was really "spent".
    • "Unconfirmed" is temporary. A new Bitcoin block arrives roughly every 10 minutes and confirms the transactions it includes, which then leave the mempool — the same rhythm as the faucet's cooldown.
    • If the connection to mempool.space drops, the tank switches to simulated fish and the status bar says so.

    Just a game

    It's play money: no sats are paid out for your score and nothing is sent to our server. Your best score is saved on this device only. Use the speaker button to mute the sounds.
