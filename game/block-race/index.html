⛏️ How Block Race works

The big idea

Bitcoin finds a new block about every 10 minutes — but only on average. Real gaps range from under a minute to over half an hour. Each block is a batch of waiting transactions that miners pick, highest fee first. Your goal: predict what the next block looks like.

What you're looking at

  • The big square is the next block, as projected live by mempool.space. Each little cell is a slice of the block's space, coloured by fee rate: blue = cheap, green / yellow = more, orange / red = paying a premium. The most expensive transactions sit at the top.
  • The two small squares are the blocks after that.
  • Falling dots are real transactions arriving right now, landing in the block they'd currently fit in. A dot too cheap for any projected block just sinks away.

How to play

  • Set your three guesses: when the block lands (counted from the moment you lock in), how many transactions it holds, and its median fee rate (the typical fee its transactions pay).
  • Press Lock in, then wait for the real block. The moment it's found, you're scored.
  • If a block lands before you lock in, the round just restarts for the next block.

Scoring (in sats)

  • Each guess earns up to 1,000 sats depending on how accurate it was. A round is worth up to 3,000 sats.
  • Accuracy is shown in plain English, e.g. "you said 4,200, actual 4,581 → 92% accurate". Being off by a factor of 2 on the fee scores 50%; being 4× off scores 0.
  • Your running total and your best round are shown top-left. It's play money — no real sats are paid out, and nothing is sent to our server.

Good to know

  • The projection is a hint, not a promise. Miners choose differently and the mempool keeps changing, so real blocks often differ. Time is pure luck: waiting longer never makes a block "due".
  • The faucet cooldown timer is shown under the squares. It's about one block long, but the game doesn't stop when it ends — it keeps going until the block is found. When the faucet is ready, go claim your sats!
  • All data comes straight to your browser from mempool.space, so nothing here loads our server (apart from one quick cooldown check).
